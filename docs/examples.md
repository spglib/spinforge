# Examples

The repository contains the notebooks used for representative paper examples.
They cover all three spin-only-group classes supported by the high-level
generator.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Collinear MnTe**

    ---

    A collinear antiferromagnetic structure in the NiAs structure type.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Open notebook](https://github.com/spglib/spinforge/tree/main/examples/paper/collinear_MnTe)

-   **Coplanar Mn₃Sn**

    ---

    A noncollinear coplanar magnetic structure on the kagome lattice.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Open notebook](https://github.com/spglib/spinforge/tree/main/examples/paper/coplanar_Mn3Sn)

-   **Noncoplanar CoTa₃S₆**

    ---

    A noncoplanar magnetic-order example in a chiral crystal structure.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Open notebook](https://github.com/spglib/spinforge/tree/main/examples/paper/noncoplanar_CoTa3S6)

</div>

The notebooks are published as source examples. Paper figures,
MAGNDATA-derived datasets, and the raw 283-material SDFT workflow are outside
the public repository.
